Before anything else

The operation costs money. It gets paid first.

We want this on the table in writing, because it was the part the last proposal buried. None of the lines below are covered by the pró-labore. They are the cost of running the bar, they come out of revenue, and nobody — you or us — sees a cent until they are all settled.

A bar that does not turn a profit in a given month pays no pró-labore beyond the R$ 12,000 and distributes nothing. There is no floor, no guarantee and no minimum owed to the founding side.

Losses stay inside the company and carry forward against future profits. No partner — including you — is ever obliged to put in additional capital. If the company needs working capital beyond its own cash flow, that is a decision for the partners, not an obligation, and whoever funds it does so as a loan repaid ahead of any profit distribution.
